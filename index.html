01 · THE WATCH

EIGHT BELLS

The bell rings eight times a day, on New York market hours. The last one, at 4:00pm, pays every holder.

9:30 open, then 10, 11, 12, 1, 2, 3, and the 4:00 close. The first seven each end a race for the biggest buy. The eighth swaps the day's fees to NVDAc and sends them to holders. On the two early close days a year the market shuts at 1:00, so there are five bells and the 1:00 bell pays.

02 · THE HOUR

THE RACE

Every hour one wallet wins: the one that bought the most $BELL, counted in NVDAc paid, and still holds it when the bell rings.

  1. 1
    BUY BIG

    From one bell to the next, every $BELL you buy adds to your total, counted in NVDAc paid. When the bell rings, the biggest total wins the hour.

  2. 2
    HOLD IT TILL THE BELL

    Only what is still in your wallet when the bell rings counts. Buy 100k and sell 100k before the bell, your score is zero. Sell half, half counts.

  3. 3
    YOUR NAME ON THE TAPE

    The winner gets 10% of the NVDAc fees collected that hour, sent to their wallet, and their name on the tape. If nobody buys in an hour, that money stays in the pot for the 4pm drop.

03 · THE CLOSE

THE DROP

At 4:00pm New York time the day's fees are swapped to NVDAc and sent to every wallet holding $BELL. It lands by itself.

Every trade of $BELL pays a 1% fee. 0.7% of that goes to the desk wallet, which our bot runs. At 4:00pm the bot swaps what came in to NVDAc and splits it between all holders, by how much $BELL each one holds. Shares under 0.00001 NVDAc wait and get added to the next day. You do not claim anything or sign anything.

$BELL is an unaudited memecoin on Base, launched on The Stonks Exchange (thestonks.exchange) and priced in NVDAc, the Coinbase tokenized Nvidia stock. It is a meme coin with a game attached. It is not a security, and this page is not financial advice.

$BELL is not Nvidia stock. Holding it gives you no claim on Nvidia Corporation, its shares, or its earnings. NVDAc is issued by Coinbase, backed by shares held in custody, and offered to non-US persons only under Regulation S. Read those terms before you trade.

The Stonks Exchange routes the creator share of trading fees, 0.7% of every trade on the pair, to the $BELL desk wallet. The platform drops nothing on holders. Our desk bot, run by us and not by the platform, swaps that share to NVDAc and sends the hourly bonus and the closing bell drop from the desk wallet. The hourly bonus goes to the biggest buyer of the hour by NVDAc paid, counting only what that wallet still holds when the bell rings. The drop goes to every holder above the dust line, split by holdings. Smaller shares roll into the next drop. Fees only exist when people trade. With no trades there is nothing to drop.

The bell follows the NYSE schedule. $BELL has no relationship with the New York Stock Exchange, Nvidia, Coinbase, or Base. The NYSE bell sound is trademarked and is not used here. The token can go to zero. Only trade what you can lose.
